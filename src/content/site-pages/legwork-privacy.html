Your documents and keys

Who reads what when you use Legwork, and who pays for it.

Who reads your documents

The worker model you connect reads the parts of your documents it searches. For research answers, your AI app, Claude or ChatGPT, receives a short sourced report. Wiki review also gives an authorized frontier model the relevant source material and proposed changes to check before publication.

Legwork shows you which worker model handled each task, so you always know where your text went.

Your keys

You connect your own account with a model provider, or a model you run yourself. You pay that provider directly. MKA Plus never resells or marks up model usage.

Running a local model

You can connect a local model for research. Wiki publication still requires authorized frontier-model review; choosing a local worker alone does not keep the entire wiki workflow on your hardware.
